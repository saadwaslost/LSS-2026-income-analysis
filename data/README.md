# Data

`adult.csv` is the UCI Adult ("Census Income") dataset: 48,842 rows, 15 columns.

Source: Becker, B. & Kohavi, R. (1996). Adult [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5XW20. Licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes from the files on the UCI page: this is the version the team used, which combines the training and test files into one, adds a header row (two columns are named `educational-num` and `gender` here, `education-num` and `sex` on UCI), and drops the spaces after commas and the full stops at the end of the test file's income labels.
